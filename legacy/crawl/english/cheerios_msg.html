Cheerios Supports the Heart and Stroke Foundation with a Special Promotion
 
Between January 12th and February 9th, with the purchase of each Cheerios' 425g box, 25 ¢ will be donated to the Heart and Stroke Foundation, up to a maximum of $20,000. The Heart and Stroke Foundation and Health Check™ thank Cheerios for their financial support through the sale of this product. This funding is used for heart and stroke research and public education work. Consumers will notice special in store advertising that promotes this fundraising program.
 
*The Heart and Stroke Foundation does not endorse this product or any other product or services of Cheerios.
