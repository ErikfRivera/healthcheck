Health Check™ Benefits

Health Check™ is a not-for-profit program established by the Heart & Stroke Foundation of Canada. Healthy eating contributes to overall health. Health Check™ makes healthy choices easier by helping you to quickly identify nutrient dense products that can contribute to a healthy and balanced diet.

When shopping for groceries, you're often faced with confusing information. Health Check™ makes it easy to make wise food choices with the simple and recognizable Health Check™ symbol displayed on food packages. And on every product that bears the Health Check™ symbol there will be a nutrient information panel and an explanatory message with more detail, including how the product relates to Canada's Food Guide to Healthy Eating and Nutrition Recommendations for Canadians. - We lead busy lives and often make quick food decisions in the grocery aisle. We want to make wise food choices, but we often lack the knowledge, time and energy to make informed decisions. As one of Canada's most authoritative health organizations, we've created a program that promotes healthy food choices as a key step which contributes to overall health.

Nutrient criteria has been developed for foods you buy most often (milk, bread, cereal, etc.) and will soon expand to many other categories including Pasta, Rice, Dairy Desserts, Legumes and Frozen meals. Keep watching for more.

The program is still very new, so companies have not had a chance to get a lot of their products into the program. This will change quickly and you will see more and more products in your supermarket over the months ahead.
