Rules & Regulations

 
HEALTH CHECK “NUTRITION MAKEOVER” CONTEST
  
THIS CONTEST IS OPEN TO CANADIAN RESIDENTS ONLY
AND IS GOVERNED BY CANADIAN LAW
 

1. HOW TO ENTER
NO PURCHASE NECESSARY. The contest begins on January 17, 2011 at 12:00:01 a.m. Eastern Time and ends on March 7, 2011 at 11:59:59 p.m. Eastern Time (the “Contest Period”). To enter the contest, visit www.healthcheck.org (the “Web Site”) and follow the directions to complete the online entry form.

Each person who enters online will receive one entry into the contest. All contest entries must be received within the Contest Period. All eligible entries received during the Contest Period will be entered in a random draw. Limit of one entry per e-mail address per day of the Contest Period. If it is discovered that you attempted to enter more times than permitted under these contest rules, all of your entries will be VOID. Entries will be rejected if entry form is not fully completed and submitted during the Contest Period.

2. ELIGIBILITY
This contest is open to all residents of Canada who have reached the age of majority in their province or territory of residence at the time of entry, except employees, representatives, agents (and those with whom such employees are domiciled) or affiliates of the Heart and Stroke Foundation of Canada (the “Sponsor”), its advertising and promotion agencies and the contest administrator.

3. PRIZES
GRAND PRIZE
There will be ten (10) Grand Prizes awarded, one per Canadian province. For the purpose of this contest, contest entries from Yukon will be added to the prize pool for British Columbia and contest entries from Nunavut and the Northwest Territories will be added to Alberta’s prize pool.  Each Grand Prize consists of economy class roundtrip airfare for two to Toronto from the national airport closest to the winner’s home (or up to the equivalent value in ground transportation if the winner lives within fifty (50) kilometres of the Greater Toronto Area, (at the sole discretion of the Sponsor), maximum value of flight should not exceed $1500.00), hotel accommodation for two nights (at a hotel selected by the Sponsor), a Health Check nutrition makeover and grocery store tour with a Heart and Stroke Foundation registered dietician at a Toronto grocery store; a heart healthy cooking class and lunch (with menu and location selected by the Sponsor); tickets to the Heart& Stroke Heart Truth Fashion Show and Reception on March 29, 2011. Bookings are subject to availability, changes in itinerary and other restrictions.  Bookings are also subject to all rules and regulations of the applicable airline or other carrier. Trip must be booked through the Sponsor and must be taken between March 28, 2011 and March 30, 2011 and cannot be modified by winner. All bookings are subject to existing availability and are subject to change. The winner and his or her guest are responsible for all other expenses that are not expressly included, including but not limited to meals, gratuities, transportation, (including without limitation ground transportation), entertainment, merchandise, souvenirs, local and long distance telephone calls, taxes (including without limitation airport taxes), travel and cancellation insurance, travel visas and all personal expenses of any kind whatsoever.  The winner and his or her guest must have all necessary identification and documents to permit travel and shall have full responsibility for obtaining and providing at their sole cost and expense all proper and legal documents and identification necessary to permit travel. It is recommended that the winner and his/her guest obtain sufficient personal insurance prior to departure. The Sponsor shall have no responsibility for trip cancellations, alterations, weather delays, weather cancellations or other changes to the trip itinerary or for anything else related to the Grand Prize or otherwise and by accepting the Grand Prize, the winner accepts such risks. The winner and his/her guest will be required to travel together, on the same itinerary. Each selected entrant and his or her guest will be required to sign a declaration and release form (see section 4 below) before the Grand Prize will be awarded.  Each guest must be of the age of majority in the jurisdiction in which he or she resides.  The approximate retail value of each Grand Prize is $4,300 CDN maximum (based on Vancouver departure).

WEEKLY PRIZES
There will be fourteen (14) Secondary Prizes awarded, each consisting of a gift basket including coupons and/or food products. Prizes will be shipped to winners’ homes.  Please allow 6 – 10 weeks following receipt by Heart and Stroke Foundation of the declaration and release form (see section 4) for delivery of the prize.  Approximate retail value of each Secondary Prize is $100 CDN.

4. DRAW AND WINNER SELECTION
GRAND PRIZE
On or about March 8, 2011 at 33 Yonge Street,Toronto, Ontario at approximately 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time, one (1) eligible entrant will be selected by random draw from all eligible entries received during the Contest Period from entrants residing in each province. The odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received in each province. 

SECONDARY PRIZE
Weekly, beginning Monday, January 24, 2011 and ending on Monday, March 7, 2011 in Toronto, Ontario at approximately 2:00pm Eastern Time, two (2) eligible entrants will be selected by random draw from all eligible entries received within the week prior.  The odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received during each of the weekly periods. Selected entrants will be contacted by phone or e-mail. If a selected entrant cannot be contacted within three (3) business days of selection or there is a return of any prior notification as undeliverable, that entrant will be disqualified and an alternate entrant will be selected from among the remaining eligible entries until such time as an entrant is contacted or there are no more eligible entries. Before being declared a winner, the selected entrant must correctly answer, unaided, a time-limited mathematical skill testing question to be administered by telephone at a prearranged mutually convenient time or e-mail.  The winners (and in the case of the Grand Prizes also their guests) are required to sign a standard declaration and release form confirming compliance with these contest rules, acceptance of the prize as awarded and releasing the Sponsor, its advertising and promotion agencies, the contest administrator and their respective officers, directors, agents, representatives, successors and assigns (the “Releasees”) from any liability in connection with this contest, the entrant’s participation therein and/or the awarding and use of the prize or any portion thereof.  If the selected entrant cannot be contacted by e-mail or telephone or fails to return the contest documents within the specified time, he/she will forfeit the prize and an alternate eligible entrant will be selected. This process will be repeated until the prize is awarded.
