Two-Bean Veggie Burgers
print this recipe send to a friend
Ingredients
  • 2 tsp (10 mL) vegetable oil
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups (500 mL) shredded carrot
  • 1 cup (250 mL) chopped mushrooms
  • 1 can (398 mL) BBQ style baked beans
  • 1 can (398 mL) dark red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup (250 mL) quick-cooking rolled oats
  • 1/4 cup (50 mL) chopped fresh parsley
  • 2 tsp (10 mL) each Worcestershire Sauce and mustard
  • 1 egg
  • 8 toasted hamburger rolls
  • Traditional hamburger condiments such as ketchup, mustard and relish
Directions
  • Heat the oil in a skillet set over medium heat.
  • Add the onion, garlic, carrot and mushroom.
  • Cook until tender and fragrant.
  • Cool to room temperature.
  • Place the baked beans, kidney beans, onion mixture, oats, parsley, Worcestershire, mustard and egg in a food processor.
  • Pulse until well combined but still chunky.
  • Transfer to a bowl and chill for 30 minutes.
  • Form the mixture into 8 patties and set on nonstick foil spread on a baking sheet.
  • Chill for 15 minutes.
  • Preheat the grill to medium.
  • Slide the nonstick foil with the patties onto the grill.
  • Cook the patties, turning once with a flexible spatula, for 7 to 8 minutes per side, or until lightly browned.
  • Transfer the burgers to toasted rolls.
  • Serve with traditional burger condiments such as ketchup, mustard and relish.
  • For a cheeseburger version, top each burger with a slice of cheddar or mozzarella cheese during the last minute of cooking.
  • For a salad supper, serve the burgers on a bed of greens mixed with chopped fresh vegetables. Drizzle with your favourite dressing.
  • For a pull-out of the freezer vegetarian solution, double the batch and freeze extra cooked burgers, individually wrapped in plastic wrap. To reheat, unwrap and microwave on High for 2 to 2 1/2 minutes, turning over once during cooking.
*Preparation time: 15 minutes (45 minutes chilling)
*Cooking time: 15 minutes
* Makes 8 burgers
Per serving:
  • patty only:
  • 189 calories
  • 9 g protein
  • 3 g fat
  • 1 g saturated fat
  • 31 g carbohydrate
  • 8 g fibre
  • 311 mg sodium

    Very high source of fibre. Excellent source of vitamin A and vitamin D. Good source of iron.
