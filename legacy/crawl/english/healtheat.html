How to Eat a Healthier Way

So...you want to eat in a healthier way. But, you are not quite sure how to go about it. Well then, this fact sheet is for you. It features Canada’s Guidelines for Healthy Eating along with some practical tips that will help you, help yourself to better health.

A Healthy Weight For You

Healthy Eating coupled with being more active is a key to achieving and maintaining a healthy weight.

Become more active every day. Begin slowly and gradually work up to a brisk walk, a more strenuous bike ride, a longer swim or more flights of stairs.
Work more physical activity into your daily routines; climb the stairs, walk at lunch, walk to the store. Find time for being more active by combining physical activity with family time such as a picnic or hike. Bike with the kids, walk the dog, skate, ski, toboggan together. Participate in activities you enjoy so that being more active is not stressful.

For more details on healthier eating ask for the other Heart and Stroke Foundation fact sheets on nutrition.

Canada’s Guidelines for Healthy Eating.

  • Enjoy a variety of foods.
  • Emphasize cereals, breads, other grain products, vegetable and fruits.
  • Choose lower fat dairy products, leaner meats and foods prepared with little or no fat.
  • Achieve and maintain a healthy body weight by enjoying regular physical activity and healthy eating.
  • Limit salt, alcohol and caffeine.

The Heart and Stroke Foundation endorses Canada’s Guidelines for Healthy Eating released by Health and Welfare Canada in March 1990.

About Salt

Most Canadians could cut back on salt by using fewer convenience foods, avoiding highly salted chips, cheeses, pretzels, nuts and crackers. Also, reducing the amount of salt used in recipes, cooking without salt, using other seasonings like pepper and herbs to flavour unsalted foods can help cut back on salt intake.
