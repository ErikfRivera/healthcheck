Delight & Lively Lobster Salad

Ingredients

  • 1 pkg (227 g) lobster flavoured surimi
  • 3 or 4 fresh mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 small yellow pepper, sliced (optional)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 tsp dried Italian seasoning
  • Lettuce leaves
  • 4 wedges fresh lemon
  • Grated Parmesan cheese (optional)

Directions

Preparation time: 15 minutes or less
Makes 4 servings.

  • Mix lobster flavoured surimi, mushrooms, pepper, olive oil and Italian seasoning.
  • Serve on lettuce leaves with a good squeeze of fresh lemon juice.
  • Top with Parmesan cheese, if desired.

Per serving:

114 Calories; 5.5 g protein; 7 g fat; 0.6 g Polyunsaturates; 5.1 g Monounsaturates; 1 g Saturates; 7 mg Cholesterol. 8.1 g carbohydrate; 0.6 g Dietary Fibre; 4 mg Calcium; 3 mg Sodium;
