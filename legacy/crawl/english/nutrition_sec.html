Nutrition Information

The Heart and Stroke Foundation is committed to improving consumers' knowledge of nutrition and eating practices.  It offers a variety of programs and materials to improve overall health through healthy eating.  Health Check, a food information program is designed to help you make wise decisions about the foods you buy.  It is based on Canada's Food Guide to Healthy Eating and promotes healthy eating in general, not just heart healthy eating.  The Health Check logo on food packages makes it easy to make wise food choices.

Heart & Stroke Healthline

The Heart and Stroke Foundation offers a free monthly e-newsletter to help Canadians live heart-healthy lives. The newsletter includes health information on heart-healthy recipes, nutrition advice, physical activity tips and assessment tools. To subscribe, click here.
