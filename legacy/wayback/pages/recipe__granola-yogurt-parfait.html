Granola Yogurt Parfait

Course/Meal: 
  • Appetizers
  • Grain-based Bars and Muffins
Main Ingredient: 
  • Grains
Prep Time: 
5 minutes
Makes: 
1 serving

For one parfait you will need 1/2 cup (125 mL) low-fat vanilla yogurt and 1/3 cup (75 mL) Homemade Apple Sauce Granola and 2 tbsp (25 mL) fresh or frozen berries. Place 2 tbsp (25 mL) of the granola into glass and top with half of the yogurt. Repeat layers once and top with remaining granola. Top with berries and serve.
 

Ingredients: 
  • 1/2 cup (125 mL) low-fat vanilla yogurt
  • 1/3 cup (75 mL) Homemade Apple Sauce Granola
  • 2 tbsp (25 mL) fresh or frozen berries
     

 

Directions: 
Place 2 tbsp (25 mL) of the granola into glass and top with half of the yogurt. Repeat layers once and top with remaining granola. Top with berries and serve.
