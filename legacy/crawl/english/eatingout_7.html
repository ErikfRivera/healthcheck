It's Easy to Join Health Check in restaurants

The Health Check program is open to all restaurants in Canada.

  1. Call or e-mail Health Check to discuss the opportunity and pre-qualify your menus.
  2. We will send you Certification Forms plus invoice for a one-time product evaluation fee.
  3. Submit the Certification Forms and evaluation fee.  The forms require product information such as size and/or ingredients.  A lab analysis may be required.
  4. We will confirm the menu item eligibility; provide all necessary menu copy (English and French), symbol and direction on graphic standards.
  5. A Licensing Agreement will be forwarded for your review and signature.
  6. Meanwhile, you can submit a menu mock-up for Health Check review.  

The process can be completed within two weeks!
