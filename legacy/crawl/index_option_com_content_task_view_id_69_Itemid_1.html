Grape Lemonberry Spritz
print this recipe send to a friend
Ingredients

  • 2 cups (500 mL) fresh raspberries

  • 1/3 cup (75 mL) lemon juice

  • 2 2/3 cups (650 mL) cold water

  • 2 1/2 cups (625 mL) Grape Juice

  • 1 cup (250 mL) sparkling water or club soda

  • crushed ice

  • fresh mint

  • fresh raspberries or purple grapes

  • lemon slices for garnish

Directions
  • Purée fresh raspberries in blender and pour into large pitcher or bowl.
  • Add lemon juice, water, grape juice and sparkling water or club soda to container.
  • Mix well.
  • Fill 8 individual glasses with crushed ice and juice mixture.
  • Garnish each glass with a fresh mint leaf, a few raspberries or purple grapes and a slice of lemon
Makes 8 servings.
Per serving:

    65 calories

    0 g fat

    0 mg sodium

    16 g carbohydrates

    2 g fibre

    1 g protein
