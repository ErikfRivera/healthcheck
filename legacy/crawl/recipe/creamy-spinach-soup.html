Creamy Spinach Soup

Course/Meal: 
  • Soups
Main Ingredient: 
  • Vegetables
Makes: 
8 cups (2 L)
Serving Size: 
1 cup (250mL)

Many creamed soups have the addition of cream, which can be high in fat. Another way to get that creamy texture is to add potatoes but here I’ve used pureed white beans to add fibre, flavour and creaminess to it. This is a tasty addition to your entertaining repertoire and can be used as a base another night for a vegetable soup by adding leftover roast veggies when warming through.

Ingredients: 
  • 1 tsp (5 mL) canola oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 stalk celery, chopped
  • 1 carrot, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp (15 mL) chopped fresh thyme or 1 tsp/5 mL dried thyme leaves
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 5 cups (1.25 L) low sodium vegetable or chicken broth
  • 1 can (19 oz/540 mL) white kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 bag (10 oz/300 g) baby spinach, trimmed and finely shredded
  • Pinch pepper
Directions: 

In soup pot, heat oil over medium heat and cook onion, celery, carrot, garlic and thyme for about 5 minutes or until softened. Add tomatoes and cook for 2 minutes. Add vegetable broth and beans; bring to boil. Reduce heat and simmer for 10 minutes.

In batches, puree soup in blender until smooth; return to soup pot. Bring soup to gentle boil and add spinach and pepper. Cook stirring, for about 5 minutes or until spinach is tender, wilted and bright green.

Nutrition Facts per serving:

Calories: 
43
Fat: 
1 g
Saturated: 
0 g
Cholesterol: 
0 mg
Sodium: 
70 mg
Potassium: 
277 mg
Carbohydrates: 
8 g
Fibre: 
2 g
Protein: 
2 g
