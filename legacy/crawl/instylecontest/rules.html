HEALTH CHECK “Healthy Food is in STYLE” CONTEST

THIS CONTEST IS OPEN TO CANADIAN RESIDENTS ONLY

AND IS GOVERNED BY CANADIAN LAW

1. HOW TO ENTER
NO PURCHASE NECESSARY. The contest begins on February 6, 2012 at 12:00:01 a.m. Eastern Time and ends on February 26, 2012 at 11:59:59 p.m. Eastern Time (the “Contest Period”). To enter the contest, visit www.healthcheck.org (the “Web Site”) and follow the directions to complete the online entry form.

Each person who enters online will receive one entry into the contest. All contest entries must be received within the Contest Period. All eligible entries received during the Contest Period will be entered in a random draw. Limit of one entry per e-mail address per day of the Contest Period. If it is discovered that you attempted to enter more times than permitted under these contest rules, all of your entries will be VOID. Entries will be rejected if entry form is not fully completed and submitted during the Contest Period.

2. ELIGIBILITY
This contest is open to all residents of Canada who have reached the age of majority in their province or territory of residence at the time of entry, except employees, representatives, or agents of the Heart and Stroke Foundation of Canada (the “Sponsor”), or of affiliates of the Sponsor (and those with whom such persons are domiciled), the Sponsor’s advertising and promotion agencies and the contest administrator.

3. PRIZES
GRAND PRIZES
There will one (1) Grand Prize awarded in each of the ten (10) Canadian provinces. Each Grand Prize consists of one (1) $1,000 Grocery Gift Card. Prizes will be shipped to winners’ homes.  Please allow 6 – 10 weeks following receipt by the Heart and Stroke Foundation of the declaration and release form for delivery of the prize.  Approximate retail value of each Grand Prize is $1,000 CDN.

SUPER GRAND PRIZE
From the ten (10) Grand Prize winners there will be one (1) Super Grand Prize winner selected by random draw. The Super Grand Prize consists of economy class roundtrip airfare for two to Toronto from the International airport closest to the winner’s home (or up to the equivalent value in ground transportation if the winner lives within fifty (50) kilometres of the Greater Toronto Area), (at the sole discretion of the Sponsor), maximum value of flight should not exceed $1500.00), hotel accommodation for two nights (at a hotel selected by the Sponsor), a Health Check grocery store tour with a Heart and Stroke Foundation registered dietitian at a Toronto grocery store; a heart healthy cooking class and tickets to The Heart Truth Fashion Show and reception on March 8, 2012. Bookings are subject to availability, changes in itinerary and other restrictions.  Bookings are also subject to all rules and regulations of the applicable airline or other carrier. Trip must be booked through the Sponsor and must be taken between March 7, 2012 and March 9, 2012 and cannot be modified by winner. All bookings are subject to existing availability and are subject to change. The winner and his or her guest are responsible for all other expenses that are not expressly included, including but not limited to meals, gratuities, transportation, (including without limitation ground transportation), entertainment, merchandise, souvenirs, local and long distance telephone calls, taxes (including without limitation airport taxes), travel and cancellation insurance, travel visas and all personal expenses of any kind whatsoever whatsoever and all other costs and/or expenses not expressly included in the Super Grand Prize.  The winner and his or her guest must have all necessary identification and documents to permit travel and shall have full responsibility for obtaining and providing at their sole cost and expense all proper and legal documents and identification necessary to permit travel. It is recommended that the winner and his/her guest obtain sufficient personal insurance prior to departure. The Sponsor shall have no responsibility for trip cancellations, alterations, weather delays, weather cancellations or other changes to the trip itinerary or for anything else related to the Super Grand Prize or otherwise and by accepting the Super Grand Prize, the winner accepts such risks. The winner and his/her guest will be required to travel together, on the same itinerary. Each selected entrant and his or her guest will be required to sign a declaration and release form (see section 4 below) before the Super Grand Prize will be awarded.  Each guest must be of the age of majority in the jurisdiction in which he or she resides.  The approximate retail value of each Grand Prize is $4,300 CDN maximum (based on Vancouver departure).

DAILY PRIZES
There will be twenty-one (21) Daily Prizes awarded, each consisting of a gift pack containing healthy eating resources. Prizes will be shipped to winners’ homes.  Please allow 6 – 10 weeks following receipt by Heart and Stroke Foundation of the declaration and release form (see section 4) for delivery of the prize. Items included in the gift pack may be altered at the Sponsor’s discretion. Approximate retail value of each Secondary Prize is $40 CDN. 

4. DRAW AND WINNER SELECTION
SUPER GRAND PRIZE and GRAND PRIZES
On or about February 27, 2012 at 33 Yonge Street, Toronto, Ontario at approximately 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time, ten (10) eligible entrants will be selected by random draw from all eligible entries received during the Contest Period from entrants residing in each province. From the ten (10) Grand Prize Winners selected, there will be a draw to select one (1) Super Grand Prize Winner. The odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received in each province. 

DAILY PRIZES
Weekly, beginning Monday, February 13, 2012 and ending on Monday, February 27, 2012 in Toronto, Ontario at approximately 2:00pm Eastern Time, one (1) eligible entrant will be selected by random draw from all eligible entries received each day during the week prior.  The odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received during each day of the contest period. Schedule for weekly draws is as follows:

Draw date From entries received
Monday, February 13, 2012 Mon., Feb. 6 – Sun., Feb 12, 2012
Monday, February 20, 2012 Mon., Feb. 13 – Sun., Feb 19, 2012
Monday, February 27, 2012 Mon., Feb. 20 – Sun., Feb 26, 2012

Selected entrants will be contacted by phone or e-mail. If a selected entrant cannot be contacted within three (3) business days of selection or there is a return of any prior notification as undeliverable, that entrant will be disqualified and an alternate entrant will be selected from among the remaining eligible entries until such time as an entrant is contacted or there are no more eligible entries. Before being declared a winner, the selected entrant must correctly answer, unaided, a time-limited mathematical skill testing question to be administered by telephone at a prearranged mutually convenient time or by e-mail.  The winners (and in the case of the Super Grand Prize also their guest) are required to sign a standard declaration and release form confirming compliance with these contest rules, acceptance of the prize as awarded and releasing the Sponsor, its advertising and promotion agencies, the contest administrator and their respective officers, directors, employees, agents, representatives, successors and assigns (the “Releasees”) from any liability in connection with this contest, the entrant’s participation therein and/or the awarding and use and/or misuse of the prize or any portion thereof.  If the selected entrant cannot be contacted by e-mail or telephone or fails to return the contest documents within the specified time, he/she will forfeit the prize and an alternate eligible entrant will be selected. This process will be repeated until the prize is awarded.
