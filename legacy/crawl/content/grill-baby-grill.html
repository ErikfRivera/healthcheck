Grill baby, grill

Posted by DPeart on 2011-06-2 12:27

When I think (dream) of the warm summer months, I picture myself in a lawn chair, sipping a cool beverage, soaking up the rays…and trying to avoid getting dinged by a stray soccer ball as I cheer from the sidelines. 

In summer, I like to be outside as much as possible, and that includes dining al fresco. Enter the storied backyard barbecue.

Part of summer fun in Canada is catching up with friends and neighbours we haven’t seen during winter hibernation. Having a barbecue is a great way to enjoy the summer weather and an excuse to get outside. Best of all, you don’t have to worry about evicting the dust bunnies that have taken up residence in your home over the winter.

Grilling is a lower fat method of cooking, which is great. For optimum flavour and nutrition, a less smoky grill is better. Heat should be high enough to cook food thoroughly, but low enough to prevent charring. 

Here are some tips for nutritious (and delicious!) barbecue fare:

  • Choose lean cuts. The health benefits of leaner cuts of meat are well documented. Choose lean cuts of red meat--loins and rounds are good choices. Cook chicken and fish more often and opt for plant-based foods and tofu to add interest--and nutrition--to the usual standards.
  • Add the antioxidants.  Try a marinade with a mixture of herbs such as thyme, red pepper, black pepper, rosemary, chives or allspice; all rich in antioxidants. Marinate for about an hour, then lightly baste meat on the grill.  Or try a rub: add herbs such as chopped rosemary directly to your meat or fish, or use a mix of olive oil and garlic.  
  • Veg out!  Red or yellow bell peppers, zucchini, even corn on the cob are delicious cooked on the grill.  Pair with grilled chicken skewers or turkey burgers and a cool, crisp summer salad and finish off with fresh juicy watermelon or other fruit for dessert.

Nutrition + flavour + minimal clean-up = happy mom! 

Try grilled pork and pineapple kabobs or spicy grilled catfish.

Cheers, and happy grilling!
