General Information
The general purpose of this site is to provide information to Canadian consumers and food companies about the Health Check program. We are continually updating the site to keep it relevant and informative.
Trademarks
Health Check and HeartSmart are trademarks of the Heart and Stroke Foundation of Canada.
Links

Linking to our Web site helps communicate valuable information to consumers about the Health Check program. Health Check was created to simplify consumers' grocery shopping experience by looking for the Health Check logo at the point of purchase.

To obtain permission to link to the Health Check Web site, please contact us at: (613) 569-4361, ext. 324. In order to facilitate this process, please provide us with a brief description of your Web site, the site's URL, and the exact location where you would like to link from your site to ours.

Once approval is granted, please use the following guidelines to establish a link to our Web site:

How to link to the Health Check program from your website:
  • Text-only links using the Health Check name in unstylized format are welcome. Your site cannot use the Health Check name, logo, or other Heart and Stroke Foundation trademarks in stylized format without prior written permission.
  • Your site can not state or imply that the Health Check program endorses a company, product or service.
  • Your site can not conflict with the mission of the Heart and Stroke Foundation.
Contact
