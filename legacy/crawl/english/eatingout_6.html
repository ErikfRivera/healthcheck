Healthy Eating Tips when away from home!

Next time you’re in one of our participating restaurants, look for the Health Check symbol on the menu. When you spot the Health Check symbol, you’ll know you’re making a healthy choice. Other restaurants will be joining the program over coming months, but in the meantime, for more information on Eating Out, read our Nutrition Brochure on Eating Out.
