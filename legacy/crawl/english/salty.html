  About Salt

Most Canadians could cut back on salt by: using fewer convenience foods; avoiding highly salted chips, cheesies, pretzels, nuts and crackers; reducing amounts used in recipes; cooking without salt; using other seasonings like pepper to flavour unsalted foods.

It is just about impossible to escape high levels of salt and sodium in most fast food meals. Healthy people can offset this sodium load by cutting way back at other meals. If you are on a salt restricted diet, don’t eat fast foods too often.
