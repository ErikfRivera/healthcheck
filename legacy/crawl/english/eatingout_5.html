Health Check™ BC Dining Program: A healthy dining program was launched this fall to help improve the overall health of British Columbians by providing healthier food choices in participating BC restaurants. The program encourages restaurants to provide healthy menu items and promote BC food products. Funding for the Health Check BC Dining Program is provided by the Province of BC and is a collaboration of the Heart and Stroke Foundation and the Government of BC.
