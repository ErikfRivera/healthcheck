So Good Rice Pudding
print this recipe send to a friend
Ingredients
  • 3 ½ cups (875 mL) plain (unflavored) soymilk

  • ¾ cup (150 mL) short grain brown rice (can be found your local health food store)

  • 1 vanilla pod
  • 1/3 cup (79mL) brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp. (30 mL)  raisins
  • 3 tbsp. (45 mL) dried cranberries
Directions
  • In a medium saucepan, stir together the soymilk and the rice

  • Split the vanilla pod lengthwise, and scrape out the seeds.

  • Add the seeds and pod to the saucepan.

  • Bring the mixture to a boil, then cover, reduce heat to medium/low and simmer. Stir occasionally. Cook until the rice is tender, approximately 60 minutes.
  • Once the rice is tender remove the pod, stir in the brown sugar, raisins, and dried cranberries.
  • Stir until it becomes creamy in consistency. Let sit covered for 15 minutes. Don't be tempted to skip the “15 minutes rest time”, it will be much more creamy if you follow this step! Stir once more and serve warm.
* Makes 4 cups 8 1/2 servings
.
Per serving:
  • 168 calories
  • 5.8 g protein
  • 2.6 g fat

  • 30 g carbohydrate
