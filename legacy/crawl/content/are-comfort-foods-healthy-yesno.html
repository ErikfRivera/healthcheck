Are comfort foods healthy? Yes/No

They can be, it depends on what a comfort food means to you. 

For some people, a comfort food is a warm cup of lentil soup or chili, while for others, a comfort food could be a cheeseburger and fries. 

If foods you find comforting are not nutrition stars, and you eat them often, consider giving them a nutrition makeover – it may be easier than you think.  For more information see Recipe Makeovers
