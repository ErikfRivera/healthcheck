Nutrient Criteria*

 

IMPORTANT NOTICE

To all Health Check Licensees/Prospective Licensees:

The Health Check nutrient criteria are based on Canada's Food Guide and government regulations. Recent updates to Canada's Food Guide may require the Heart and Stroke Foundation to initiate a number of revisions to the criteria. The nutrient criteria are currently being reviewed by Health Check's Technical Advisory Committee made up of dietitians and nutritional experts. You will be notified of any changes to the criteria in your respective food category by September 30th, 2007. As per the Licensing Agreement, all Health Check Licensees will have 9 months (until June 30th, 2008) to ensure all products meet the revised nutrition criteria.

 

The Health Check Program is based on Canada's Food Guide to Healthy Eating. Different food categories have different nutrient criteria. The criteria are defined using Health Canada's Nutrient Content Claims. Definitions of the claims are included in a separate document called "Nutrient Claims".

All products must meet the Health Check criteria for the specific Health Check Serving size and the on-package serving size. The team of Health Check Registered Dietitians are happy to pre-qualify your product to ensure it meets the Health Check nutrient criteria.

Sodium Values are evaluated for all categories. The criteria used for evaluation is based on the values from the Heart Health Claim (480 mg for single foods and 960 mg for entrees).Sodium values are evaluated on 50g for any serving that has a Health Check serving size/on package serving size of 30g or less.

Low Fat claim is evaluated on 50g for any serving that has a Health Check serving size/on package serving size of 30g or less.
