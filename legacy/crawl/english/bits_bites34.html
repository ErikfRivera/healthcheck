A. Health Check is now four years old!

B. More licensees and products join Health Check.

A. :::: Health Check is now four years old! ::::

Four years ago, the Heart and Stroke Foundation launched the Health Check program nationally. More and more Canadians are becoming aware of this simple tool to make wise food choices when they shop. The program has grown steadily and now includes some 60 licensees and more than 350 products, and new products are being added every month. A good part of this success is due to the contribution of our licensees who are incorporating Health Check in their advertising plans and are requesting that the Health Check PSAs be positioned adjacent to their products when placing their media buy. Thanks to strong media support our public service announcements are reaching millions of Canadians and our Web site continues to attract thousands of visitors every month.
