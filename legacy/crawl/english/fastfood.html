Fast Food

On average, Canadians eat out once a week at a fast food restaurant. Although many items offered are high in calories and fat, your choices can make all the difference!

The next time you order fast food, try these fat cutting strategies:

Order basic menu items such as: a regular hamburger instead of a deluxe-double patties-cheeseburger-with-bacon; a regular pizza instead of one with double cheese and extra bacon.

Order a charbroiled chicken sandwich instead of one that’s breaded and fried; have a taco instead of a burger; try chili for a change.

When you place your order make it clear that you don’t want any special burger sauces or squirts of mayonnaise on your order.

Enjoy juice, 1% or 2% milk instead of a milk shake.

Choose a low calorie salad dressing - it cuts down on fat big time!

For breakfast have cereal, an English or lower-fat muffin with jam or hot cakes or pancakes with syrup only. Avoid the typical breakfast sandwiches of egg, bacon and cheese.

Skip fast food pies, turnovers, cookies and ice cream bars - they’re really high in fat.
