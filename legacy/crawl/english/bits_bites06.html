A. :::: News Flash: Stonemill Bakehouse Ltd. Joins Health Check™ ::::

Welcome to Stonemill Bakehouse Ltd., the latest company to join the Health Check™ Program. Look for select Stonemill Bakehouse breads and bagels in stores throughout Ontario beginning in the month of May.

B. :::: New CPR manuals not just about saving hearts ::::

As a result of new CPR guidelines, the Heart and Stroke Foundation’s CPR training manuals, including Heart Saver, will soon be released to all provincial Foundations for distribution to trainers. The healthy eating section features the Health Check™ logo and a brief description of the Program. All English and French Heart Saver, Basic Rescuer manuals and other related resources will include this special Health Check™ piece. The annual print run is about 500,000 pieces!

C. :::: Health Check™ tagged to Welchs’ Grape Juice TV ad ::::

Mott's Canada has produced a new 30-second TV ad for Welchs' Grape Juice which includes a 5-second tag for Health Check™: "Welch's grape juice is a proud participant in the Heart and Stroke Foundation's Health Check™ Program. So, you know you've made a good choice." Watch for it!
