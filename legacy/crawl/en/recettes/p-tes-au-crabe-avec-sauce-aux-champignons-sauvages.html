Pasta with Crab and Wild Mushroom Sauce
print this recipe send to a friend
Ingredients
  • 3 cups (750 mL) rotini pasta
  • 1 tbsp (15 mL) olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 8 oz (250 mL) mushrooms (white, cremini, shiitake)), sliced
  • 1 pkg (8 oz/227 g) crab flavoured surimi fish, flake style
  • 3/4 cup (175 mL) dry white wine
  • 1 1/2 cups (375 mL) cut green beans (fresh or frozen)
  • 1/4 tsp (1 mL) each dried thyme leaves and salt
  • 1/2 cup (125 mL) light sour crean
  • 1 tbsp (15 mL) all purpose flour
Directions
  • Cook pasta according to package directions. Drain.
  • Meanwhile, heat oil over medium-high heat in large skillet. Sauté garlic, onion and mushrooms 5 minutes. Stir in crab, wine, beans, thyme and salt; bring to boil and simmer 2 minutes until beans are just tender.
  • Mix sour cream and flour; add to skillet. Cook and stir until thickened. Spoon over hot pasta. Serve with grated Parmesan cheese, if desired.

    • Hint: Use a variety of wild mushrooms for interesting flavour and texture. When using shiitake mushrooms, discard the woody stems.

    A simple one dish pasta sauce that cooks the same time as the pasta. Ideal for entertaining.

* Preparation time: 20 minutes
* Cooking time: 8 minutes
* Makes 4 servings.
Per serving:
  • Energy 327Cal
  • 15 g Protein
  • 6 g Fat
  • 14 mg Cholesterol
  • 50 g Carbohydrate
  • 4 g Dietary Fibre
  • 581 mg Sodium
