B. :::: GPC International Leads Major Health Check™ Marketing Initiative ::::

Health Check™ is pleased to announce that GPC International, a strategic public affairs and communications consulting company, will be managing the Health Check™ marketing plans for 2001/2002. GPC has considerable experience working with Health Check™, The Heart and Stroke Foundation and other non-profit associations such as the CPMA (creators of the 5-10 a day campaign). Find out more in the coming weeks by reading the next edition of Health Check™ Update.


C. :::: In-Store promotion coming this January ::::

This year’s In-Store event will be bigger and better. Health Check™ is working with licensees on a special campaign to distribute shelf advertising in 1,883 grocery stores and cart advertising in 1,261 grocery stores nationally. The campaign will run from December 31, 2001 to January 27, 2002 for four consecutive weeks. More details to come in future editions.

D. :::: Cheerios Mother-Daughter Walk - September 23 ::::

The Cheerios Mother Daughter Walk for Heart and Stroke is taking place on September 23 in more than 51 cities across Canada. Visit www.heartandstroke.ca for more information or call 1-888-473-4636. Please note there are no sites in the province of Quebec.
