It's Easy to Join Health Check...
  1. Call or e-mail Health Check to discuss the opportunity and pre-qualify your products
  2. We will send you Certification Forms plus invoice for a one-time product evaluation fee.
  3. Submit the Certification Forms and evaluation fee.  The forms require product information such as SKU number, size and ingredients.   A lab analysis may be required.
  4. We will confirm the product's eligibility; provide all necessary package copy (English and French), Logo and direction on graphic standards.
  5. A Licensing Agreement will be forwarded for your review and signature. The Licensing Fee is required at this time.
  6. Meanwhile, you can submit a label mock-up for Health Check review.   Our nutritionists will verify that the three on-pack elements (Logo, Explanatory Message and Nutrition Facts Table) are correct.

How long will it take?  We can complete the process within two weeks!
