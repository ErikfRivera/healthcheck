Potato, Tomato & Egg Pie
in a Pan
print this recipe send to a friend
Ingredients
  • 1 tsp (5mL) soft non-hydrogenated margarine
  • 3 medium red skin or new potatoes, sliced 1/4-inch (6mm) thick
  • 1/3 cup (75 mL) chopped fresh basil
  • 8 eggs
  • 1/2 cup (50 mL) skim milk
  • 2 tbsp (30 mL) Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp (5 mL) dry mustard
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 2 medium Italian tomatoes, sliced 1/4-inch (6 mm) thick
Directions
  • Lightly coat a 10-inch (25 cm) non-stick skillet with soft non-hydrogenated margarine. Heat over medium heat. Place half of potato slices, in one layer, in hot skillet. Cover and cook 3 minutes or until potato slices are lightly brown. Turn potato slices over; cover and cook 3 more minutes or until lightly brown. Remove from skillet. Cook remaining potato slices. Remove from skillet.*
  • Overlap all cooked potato slices, in the bottom and up the sides of the skillet.
  • Sprinkle with basil. Whisk together eggs, milk and mustards in a large bowl. Season with salt and pepper. Pour over potato slices in skillet. Place tomato slices over egg mixture. Cover and cook over medium-low heat for 10-12 minutes or until eggs are set, lifting the edges to allow uncooked egg to run to bottom of skillet.
  • Cut into wedges to serve.
* Recipe can be prepared ahead of time up to this point.
*Preparation time: ?
* Makes 4 servings.
Per serving:
  • 267 Calories
  • 16 g protein
  • 12 g fat
  • 24 g carbohydrate
