The Heart and Stroke Foundation's Health CheckTM Program
Launches New TV Public Service Announcement (PSA)

The problem of obesity is growing in Canada, and part of the solution is helping Canadians make healthier food choices with Health Check!

The Health Check campaign continues with a new TV PSA. In this spot, a Foundation dietitian helps our shopper make healthy food choices. But unlike our last TV spot where dietitians were literally in the shopping cart - this time she's hiding where the real action is - behind the grocery shelf!   This new PSA called "Store Shelves" is a humorous look at our shopper who receives a little friendly push from the Foundation's dietitian. Healthy choices seem to be falling right off the shelf!

With the help of our media friends the Heart and Stroke Foundation can cut through the information clutter and confusion and make it easier to shop for healthy choices.

For more information on this campaign or to receive a BETA or other format dub of these PSAs, please contact Anna Rubaj at 613-569-4361 ext 345 or arubaj@hsf.ca.
